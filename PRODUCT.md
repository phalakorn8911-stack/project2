# Product

## Register

product

## Users
7 User Roles: Admin, Vehicle Officers (เจ้าหน้าที่ยานยนต์), Drivers (พลขับ), Mechanics (ช่างซ่อม), Head Mechanics (หัวหน้าช่าง), Parts Inventory Officers (เจ้าหน้าที่คลังอะไหล่), Commanders/Executives (ผู้บังคับบัญชา).
Context: Military logistics and maintenance. Users need to track vehicle status, approve repairs, execute maintenance tasks, and check parts availability.

## Product Purpose
A comprehensive "Smart Army Vehicle Maintenance Dashboard" to manage the lifecycle of military vehicles, track maintenance schedules, handle repair requests and work orders, and manage spare parts inventory. Success looks like having a single source of truth for all vehicle statuses, reducing missed maintenance schedules, and clear cost tracking for repairs.

## Brand Personality
Professional, authoritative, reliable, structured, and efficient.
Voice: Clear, objective, and action-oriented.

## Anti-references
- Playful or overly colorful consumer apps.
- Cluttered, dense data tables without visual hierarchy.
- Startup/SaaS templates with unnecessary gradients or "glassmorphism" that distract from the data.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over decoration:** Data visibility is paramount. Use space and typography to group information.
- **Status at a glance:** Use a consistent, semantic color system for statuses (e.g., Available, In Repair, Overdue) so users can instantly parse the state of a vehicle or work order.
- **Efficient execution:** Forms and data entry (like work orders and inspections) should be optimized for speed, especially on mobile devices for mechanics and drivers.
- **Progressive disclosure:** Don't overwhelm users with 25 fields at once; show primary info and let them drill down.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast for legibility, especially on mobile devices outdoors (for drivers/mechanics).
- Clear touch targets for mobile interfaces (forms and inspection checklists).
- Standard web accessibility for forms and tables.
